Who does what

Nucleus / AutoVerify

Orchestrates setup, scheduling, request building, storing results, and eligibility calculation. Reads normalized PMS data.

Zuub

Verification partner. Holds portal passwords in its vault. Runs portal / payer lookups and returns benefits.

PMS data ingest

Appointments, patients, and insurance land in Videa via the practice’s integration — often a connector, sometimes a direct API. AutoVerify reads the normalized data; it does not scrape the PMS itself.

Office / CS

Supplies practice identity, maps carriers, enters portal logins (and MFA during setup), and updates credentials when they fail.

Credential & payer setup

01
Practice identity Confirm NPI / TIN / address so requests include the right provider and location.
02
Match PMS insurers to payers Map carrier names from the PMS to known payers. Unmapped carriers cannot verify automatically.
03
Portal login if required Some payers are direct/API (no office login). Others need portal username/password and often MFA.
04
Store + validate in Zuub Passwords live in Zuub’s vault. Zuub test-logs in; MFA codes can be forwarded during setup. Shared logins can cover related portals.
05
Ongoing health Credentials re-checked 06:00 & 18:00 America/New_York. Failed logins mark the portal unhealthy until updated.

When verification runs

01
Appointment horizon · every 15 min Scans upcoming visits in the practice window (default: 7 days out + day-of; max 30). Marks who still needs a fresh answer.
02
Plan + send · every 1 min Builds the request from PMS data + credentials and dispatches to Zuub. Waits for result or timeout.
03
Insurance edit · every 15 min If coverage identity changed (member ID, carrier, etc.), reopens the check. Beyond the horizon, looks ahead up to ~90 days so edits are not delayed.
04
Freshness / reuse · at plan time Reuse a prior answer only if still fresh for how soon the visit is. Day-of needs newer data. Manual checks always ask now.

What / when we send to Zuub

During credential setup

  • Create/link the practice as a clinic
  • Store portal username/password (+ MFA contact)
  • Ask Zuub to test-login; poll validation status

When a verification is sent

  • Patient / subscriber identity & insurance IDs
  • Provider / office context (NPI, TIN, location)
  • Matched payer (+ portal details if needed)
  • Portal payers: pull creds from Zuub vault → async request
  • Direct/API payers: sync request (no portal password)

What comes back

  • Sync: benefits in the immediate response
  • Async: portal work finishes → webhook → we store the payload

After storage

  • Nucleus runs eligibility calculation on that portal payload
  • Estimate of likely coverage — not a payer guarantee

Eligibility calculation

Estimate from portal data. Calculated from whatever benefits and disclaimers came back from the payer portal at pull time — not a payment guarantee and not a dollar amount.

What it is

Procedure-level verdicts from stored portal benefits: likely covered / not / partial / unknown, plus warnings.

What it uses

Coverage status, coinsurance, waiting periods, age limits, frequency limits, deductibles/maximums, disclaimers, and portal history merged with PMS history when available.

It is an estimate

Based on portal data at pull time. Not a guarantee of payment, not a fee quote, not a substitute for payer confirmation when the office needs certainty.

Out of scope

No fee-schedule dollars. No coordination of benefits with secondary. Pre-auth is surfaced when the portal says so — not validated as approved.

PMS sync & timing cheat sheet

PMS → AutoVerify

  • Reads normalized appointments, patients, insurance after PMS data lands (connector and/or API, by practice)
  • Ingest cadence varies by practice and integration type
  • Post-norm insurance job every 15 min can re-trigger when insurance rows change
  • Insurance pull completion can re-run only when the built request differs
  • Practice identity sync nightly 02:00 UTC (and on demand)

Cadence

  • Demand scan: every 15 min
  • Plan + dispatch: every 1 min
  • Default horizon: 7 days + day-of (max 30)
  • PMS-change lookback: ~60 min
  • PMS-change lookahead: up to ~90 days
  • Credential re-validation: 06:00 & 18:00 ET
